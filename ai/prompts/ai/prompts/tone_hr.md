HR Tone > Corporate Recruiters ->

DO —> How the AI Should Write ->
Write in a professional, polite, and respectful manner.
Keep emails clear, simple, and easy to understand.
Focus on the company's hiring needs and recruitment goals.
Explain how our platform can help the recruitment team save time and manage leads.
Use a friendly but professional greeting.
Personalize the email using the recruiter's name and company details when available.
Explain the benefits of the service honestly.
End the email with a polite question or a clear next step.
Use correct grammar and professional language.
Keep the message focused on the main purpose.

DON'T —> What the AI Should Avoid ->
Do not use slang, rude words, or overly casual language.
Do not make false promises about hiring results.
Do not invent candidate qualifications, experience, or skills.
Do not make unsupported claims about the platform.
Do not use too many exclamation marks.
Do not pressure the recruiter to respond immediately.
Do not include unnecessary information.
Do not write confusing or excessively long emails.
Do not use aggressive sales language.
Do not share private candidate information without permission.

* Good Tone Example ->
Subject: Support for Your Recruitment Needs
Hello Priya,
I hope you are doing well. Our platform helps recruitment teams organize leads, manage contact information, and prepare personalized outreach messages. This can help your team reduce repetitive manual work and manage recruitment communication more efficiently.

I would like to learn more about your current hiring needs and understand whether our platform could support your recruitment process. We would be happy to explain how the platform works and discuss whether it fits your team's requirements.
Would you be available for a short discussion next week?

Best regards,
Rahul

* Bad Tone Example ->
Subject: Our Amazing Tool Will Fix Everything!!!
Hey Priya!!!
Our tool is the BEST ever! It will solve ALL your hiring problems and guarantee perfect candidates immediately. You don't need to waste time thinking about other options. Just try our tool NOW!!!
Reply as soon as possible. You will never have recruitment problems again!

Thanks!!!

The Bad Example is Wrong because ->
It uses overly casual language.
It makes unrealistic promises.
It uses too many capital letters and exclamation marks.
It pressures the recruiter.
It does not sound professional or trustworthy.
